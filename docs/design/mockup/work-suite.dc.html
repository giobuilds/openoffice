Standing orders placed before the 20th of the month keep the old rate for a further quarter. Accounts settled within fourteen days keep the 2% early-settlement discount; accounts beyond thirty days will move to prepay, which we would rather not do to anybody.

We are a bakery of four people. Every rise here is a cost we have already paid — none of it is margin.
